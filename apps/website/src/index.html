A worked example

One purchase. One repayment.

You spend ₹1,000 at a bookshop and pay your card from your bank account. The purchase counts toward spending. The repayment moves money between your accounts.

Try this in Coinbox →
1. Check the two statements

The bank shows a ₹1,000 withdrawal on 6 September. The card shows a ₹1,000 payment received on 7 September, alongside the bookshop purchase.

Download sample bank CSVDownload sample card CSV
2. Match the repayment

In Import Inbox, compare the withdrawal with the payment received. Choose Match as transfer and save your decisions once you’ve checked the other rows.

3. Check your spending

Overview shows ₹1,000 in net spending and ₹5,000 in income for these statements. The Ledger shows the transfer, and you can open its two statement references.
